# Review by Omar Haddad — Delivery Driver, Learning English

I like this course. The 3D buildings are clear—I can see exactly how the doors sit and how numbers flow. The colors help: green doors stand out, arrows show which way to read. Good design. But when I try to learn, some words trip me up. Here is what I see.

## The buttons say different things

One moment I click "Continue ▸", next moment I click "Ready ▸", then "Next Building ▸". Why three words? In my delivery job, every button does one clear thing. A driver needs consistency. Is the difference between "Continue" and "Ready" real, or just words? 

For someone learning English, this is confusion. **Suggestion**: pick one word (maybe "Next ▸" always) or make the meaning obvious: "Continue to Quiz" and "Ready to Test" are clearer.

## The prompt words are too long and have idioms I don't know

Part 1 says: "memorize the pattern, then on the next buildings you will fill in the missing unit numbers."

What does "memorize" mean in this context? It is a big word. Does it mean "study it" or "remember it by heart"? When I deliver, I read the number fast—I don't memorize the whole building.

Part 2 says: "keep the pattern going." This phrase is English idiom. A new speaker does not know what "going" means here. **Better**: "follow the same pattern" or "use the same pattern."

Part 5 is very long—one sentence has four bold words and parentheses:
> "Building 6 runs 601 → 620, so floor 1 holds the **lowest** numbers *and* the **highest** (617-620)"

This is hard to parse. The parentheses are not standard in teaching. And "no digit tells you the floor"—this negative phrasing makes me think. **Better**: "The floor is not in the number" or "No floor digit in the unit number."

## The quiz panel has one unclear word

It says: "Pick the number that fits the pattern."

What does "fits" mean? Does the door fit the number? The number fits the pattern? For ESL, this is ambiguous. **Better**: "Pick the number that matches the pattern" or "Pick the correct number."

## Mobile: Part 5 prompt overflows

I tested on a phone (390px wide). The prompt at the top gets cut off—text disappears under the screen edge. On a small phone, I cannot read the full instruction. The prompt width looks fixed, but the screen is tiny.

**The text that gets cut**:
- "keep the pattern going." (line wrap hides this)
- "Building 6 runs 601 → 620" (hides)
- "Use the Front / Back / Left / Right" (hides or wraps awkwardly)

The "Walk around" buttons on the left side are good—they take up space but they're needed for Part 5. But the prompt above should shrink or break into two lines.

## Part 3: Building label shows "#" instead of number in quiz

In the quiz panel, the prompt says: "Building B — fill in the missing unit numbers, then name the building."

But in the screenshot, it shows "Building #" as a placeholder. This looks like a bug, not a design choice. The learner should see "Building B" or the actual building number (like "1", "2", "3", "4", "5", or "6" depending on the stage). A question mark (#) makes me think something is missing.

## The yellow/amber text is good—but check readability

The bold amber text (`#ffd9a0`) on dark background is readable on a desktop screen. On my phone, it is still clear. Good color choice. The background blur is subtle and does not distract. ✓

## The 3D graphics communicate well

The arrows in Part 1 show reading order—this is visual and instant. I do not need words to understand "go this way." The floor badges in Part 2 (the big yellow numbers 1, 2, 3, 4) are perfect. The "Building X" banner above the roof in Part 3—I immediately understand that this is the building number. No translation needed.

The Part 5 "Walk around" navigation lets me orbit the building myself. When I drag, the camera moves smoothly. When I click the Front/Back/Left/Right buttons, the view snaps clear. This is good interaction—I feel in control.

## One small thing: "partial number" is not defined

The prompts say "click a door marked ?" or "click a door showing a partial number." What is a "partial number"? On screen I see "71_5" or "7 07". Is that "partial"? The word is not explained. For a learner, one example in the prompt would help: "like 71_5, with the middle digit missing" or "like partial: 1_02."

## Summary

**What works**:
- 3D buildings are clear and beautiful
- Arrows and labels make the pattern visual—no guessing
- Quiz panel is organized and the colors (green for right, red for wrong) are universal
- Navigation buttons (Front/Back/Left/Right in Part 5) are intuitive

**What needs fixing**:
1. **Consistency of button text** — pick one action word
2. **Simplify the prompts** — shorter sentences, avoid idioms like "keep the pattern going," explain words like "memorize"
3. **Quiz header wording** — change "fits" to "matches" (clearer for ESL)
4. **Fix Part 3 quiz prompt** — show actual building label, not "#"
5. **Part 5 mobile text overflow** — the prompt wraps awkwardly on phones
6. **Define "partial number"** — add one example in the text

I am ready to take this course. I just want the words to be simpler and the same each time.
