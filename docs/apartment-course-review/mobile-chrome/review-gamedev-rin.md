# Game-design review: pacing of the Stop Finder apartment course, and what to build

Played hands-on in the Browser pane, Classic look: desktop 1280×720 (Parts 1–2 complete, Part 3 Building B through the roof sign, Part 4/5 openers with a drag and a walk), portrait 375×812 touch (Part 1 Buildings 2–3, Part 4 opener, Sunbelt Part 4 opener), landscape 740×360 touch (Building 2 with a deliberate miss, Part 5 opener with a walk). The pane's "desktop" preset is a 485×1004 portrait pane inside the phone query, so I emulated 1280×720 explicitly. Values are CSS px.

## 1. Pacing verdict

**Beat chart** (opener → first quiz → ramp → completion):

- **Part 1.** Opener: one action (Next), a 1.4 s intro swing, the arrows teach. First quiz (B2): tap `?` → pick → done; three inputs to the first win, ~5 s on desktop. Ramp: B3 two blanks, B4 three — the cleanest 1-2-3 ramp in the course, each blank the same 2 s operation. Completion: byte-identical to a building completion (same 170-piece burst, same "Done ✓"); the only part-boundary signal is the copy "Next: Part 2 ▸".
- **Part 2.** Opener: tower with the floor column, reads in one look. First quiz (B): the same three-beat. Ramp: C jumps 1 → 4 blanks, fine because the operation is identical. A miss is instant and specific ("Right column, wrong floor — the first digit is the floor.") and the option stays listed. Best beat in the course.
- **Part 3.** Opener: roof sign + place key. B: four partials then the building; C: nine partials then the building — ten questions, the first building that feels long. The **roof-sign step is a zero-click beat**: after the last door the panel swaps in place to "Which building is this?" (1 / 71 / 17 / 7). Good pacing, no dead air — but the prompt then says "Now click the roof sign", an instruction for something that already happened.
- **Part 4.** Opener: the block plus the first mandatory camera verb (drag to spin); the quiz building has 13 blanks, the biggest in the course. Desktop: the spin is good, back numbers read. Portrait: the block is ~125 px tall with 20×8 px plates, unplayable; Sunbelt portrait the camera is inside a neighbour — a stucco wall and one window (confirmed).
- **Part 5.** Opener: the three-quarter view shows two faces at once — the concept in one frame. But the 350 ms walk interpolates position on a straight line, and Front → Back passes **through** the building: a full-frame wall flash mid-move. The reset chip also appears after a deliberate walk (`driftedFromPose` compares against the stage pose, not the chosen view).

**Time-to-first-interaction.** Desktop ≈ 2 s; the first `?` is one click and ~5 s in. Excellent. Portrait: same count, but the target is a 30×14 px plate on a 110 px building, the first-run drag hint sits on that building for 7 s, and the 10 s idle tip re-arms on every quiz building, slides in at 46% across the plates it names, and stays up after the sheet opens (seen on B3).

**Reward timing.** The plate pop + burst (500 ms, at the plate) is the right reward and is in the gaze path everywhere. The building confetti is not: it spawns at `innerHeight × 0.32`, the roofline on desktop (fine), but in portrait y≈260 over the jump bar — 300+ px from the sheet — and in landscape y≈115 over the prompt while the eye is in the right column. Worse, on phones the sheet never auto-closes after a tapped answer (`:hover` sticks, the 1100 ms close aborts; reproduced in portrait and landscape at 2.6 s), so the last correct answer ends with "Done ✓" top-right, Next hidden under the sheet, and the prompt still saying "Click a door marked ?". The completion beat ends in a hunt for ×. On desktop with keys the same beat is tight: burst, green, Next, closed by 1.5 s. Confetti lives ~3 s and follows a fast player into the next stage; three times I saw the previous building's rain over a new opener.

**Interruption cost of the chrome.** Portrait idle: 363 of 812 px is frame, the building 14%. The prompt repeats the same instruction every building. The amber Home pill out-shouts Next, a smaller green pill beside it — the exit outranks the primary action. Landscape: the long body (28% of the height) overlaps the roofline, the column clips option d and hides the miss reason, the Part 5 minimap sits on the roof.

**Rating: 6/10** — the loop (tap → pick → instant verdict → pop) is right and desktop plays at an 8; on phones the framing, the stuck sheet and off-gaze rewards turn every completion into dead time, a 4.

## 2. Critique of the two reviews

- **Prompt folds to its title (phones, quiz stages) — SHIP.** Dara keeps the body only on the run's first quiz (stage 1); Hollis on the first quiz building of each part. **Pick Hollis**: Parts 3 and 4 introduce new verbs (partials; drag to spin, hidden doors) and Dara's rule folds exactly those. Stages 1, 5, 8, 11, 13 keep the `short` body until their first `quizopen`; other quiz stages are title-only. Tap-to-expand: Dara's toggle, no 8 s timer (timed text taxes slow readers), re-fold on the next `quizopen`. Hollis's auto-expand on the second miss: **reject** — the rule is already appended to the sheet message. Desktop keeps the body. Fix both copy bugs: `short` under the landscape query, re-run `setPrompt` on resize.
- **Jump bar → progress strip with a timed peek — SHIP with changes.** Peek per building (Dara) vs per part (Hollis): **pick per part.** Five buttons unfolding every twenty seconds is animation for its own sake and invites the mis-taps the players feared; the segment ticking green is the per-building feedback (Dara's own argument). Dwell 3 s, not 4, started on `quizclose`. Strip: Dara's 28 px row ("Part 2 · 5/14" + 14-segment bar + caret, 44 px hit via `::after`); Hollis's 8 px bar is too thin to read as a control. Ask 3 means `#jumpbar` only, never `#bottombar` — all three of us agree, and I watched the course dead-end when Next was covered.
- **Home + Appearance into one button — SHIP.** ⌂ (Hollis) vs ⋯ (Dara); first-quiz animated collapse (Hollis) vs from stage 0 (Dara). **Pick ⋯, from stage 0, no collapse animation.** A house glyph on a menu button lies (it reads as "exit"); nobody watches chrome shrink; one rule per layout keeps the four files identical. Amber Home lives inside the menu — and with no amber on the surface, green Next becomes the loudest thing at the bottom, fixing the hierarchy inversion for free.
- **Per-type portrait framing + view offset — SHIP Dara's spec** (extents, `FILL_W 0.84`, `FILL_H 0.9`, distances plex 24 / tower 30 / row 40 / block 30 / seq 40 at 375×812, cap 1.55×, `setViewOffset` against a `safeRect` band); Hollis's targets (55–65% of the free height, plate ≥22 px tall) are the acceptance test. One change: apply the offset on `stagechange`, `quizopen`, `resize`, but **do not tween it back on `quizclose`** — the picture would bob on every answer and the reward needs a still frame; hold it until the next `stagechange` or a drag. Dara's optional dolly per tap: **reject** — thirteen camera moves on a 13-blank building; ≥45 px plates are enough. Sunbelt neighbour move + `DECOR_BOXES` guard: ship.
- **Sticky-hover auto-close fix — SHIP, P0.** The biggest single pacing fix in the pile. Hollis's extra Next in the sheet footer: **reject** — two Nexts is a smell; once the sheet closes at 1.1 s the bottom bar's Next is where the eye drops. Keyboard users keep Escape.
- **2×2 options in landscape — SHIP**, with `#qmsg` moved under the heading. At 740×360 the column clips option d and the miss reason is below the fold; I picked wrong on purpose and saw red with no explanation.
- **Hint placement — SHIP Hollis's three** (bottom-anchored portrait, under the prompt landscape; tip once per session; "Tap" wording) plus: hide the tip on `quizopen`.
- **Confetti origin — SHIP Hollis's** (emit from the solved plate; keep the cannons) and promote P2-5 to must-do: clear on `stagechange`.
- **Stale "Building done" prompt — SHIP** (both): title "Building 2 ✓", no body. Also `nameBuilding`: drop "click the roof sign".
- **Touch snap / larger halo — SHIP** the 1.3× halo on `(pointer: coarse)` and add a snap (§4).
- **`body.quiz-open` on phones (Dara P1) — SHIP**, chip visually hidden but live. **Part 5 chrome** (Hollis P2-2/3) and **copy notes** 1–4 (Hollis §6): ship.

## 3. The players

All three report doors that "would not click". I hit the identical symptom on desktop with a mouse — and the panel had opened on the first click every time: the pane's screenshot lags one capture behind the action, so a tester clicks, sees nothing, clicks elsewhere, and the third screenshot catches up. Perpetua's coordinates (240–260, 373–395) are 50 px right of the B2 plate at (196,364) — the window. Zippy's (370–377,155) on B4 landscape is the slab between the rows (plates at y≈130 and 190). Marrow's (189,372) is inside the plate-or-door zone and almost certainly opened it. **The "won't register" diagnosis is tester artefact.** The target-size complaint underneath is real and I felt it: 30×14 px plates for a 44 px thumb, 20×8 on the tower and block, a 6 px tap slop a rolling thumb exceeds. The fix is framing + slop + snap + halo, not raycasting.

Changed my mind: Zippy's "landscape is already compact; the problem is targets, not space" — right except for the prompt and the column, so the landscape strip should stay modest. Perpetua's "keep the part buttons visible" — a fair warning that the strip must read as a control ("Part 2" + caret) and the bottom bar is sacred. Marrow's "collapse after the first quiz building of each part" is the rule I chose.

Wrong: Marrow's "hit Part 2 aiming for Next" — 600 px apart in portrait, 450 in landscape. Perpetua's "portrait wastes no space" — 45% chrome; she never passed B2, so her "Ease 2" rates the tool. Zippy's "sub-millimetre tower doors" — modest in landscape (≈26×10 px), where the framing fix does not apply; halo and snap are what help him.

## 4. My additions

1. **Close the sheet, then reveal Next where the eye is** (the hover fix closes it) and let nothing on the surface outrank Next — free with ⋯. Pacing: a completion must end in the next input, not a search.
2. **A real part-completion beat.** On the last quiz of a part (stages 3, 6, 9, 11, 13) fire the cannons instead of the burst, expand the strip 3 s with the next part's button pulsing once, keep "Next: Part N ▸". Pacing: the ramp needs a crest every three buildings or the 14-building run flattens.
3. **Confetti hygiene.** Clear on `stagechange`, 90 pieces on phones; nothing should still be falling when the next opener asks to be read.
4. **Camera arcs, not chords.** Part 5: interpolate the azimuth about the target so the building never fills the frame mid-walk; no reset chip while a `SEQ_VIEWS` view is active. Pacing: the walk is the Part 5 pacing device; a wall flash reads as a glitch.
5. **Touch tolerance.** Tap slop 12 px for `pointerType === 'touch'` (6 for mouse); a touch tap that hits nothing snaps to the unsolved blank whose projected plate centre is within 28 px; halo 1.3×. Pacing: a missed tap is a silent failure whose only feedback is a tip 10 s later.

## 5. Final spec (priority order)

1. **Auto-close fix, all layouts.** `armAutoClose`: `reading = (matchMedia('(hover: hover)').matches && ui.quiz.matches(':hover')) || ui.quiz.contains(document.activeElement)`. Keep 1100 ms.
2. **Framing.** `EXTENTS` + `fitDistance()` per Dara, cap 1.55×; `safeRect()` = below `#prompt` (+strip), above the sheet when open, else above `#viewbar`/`#bottombar`; `setViewOffset` on phone layouts on `stagechange`, `quizopen`, `resize`, held through `quizclose`; 250 ms ease, cut under reduced motion. Sunbelt neighbour to x = 42; `DECOR_BOXES` → `controls.maxDistance = firstHit − 3` on `stagechange`. Acceptance at 375×812: plex ≈ 315×170 px, block ≈ 300×255 px, 5-digit plates ≥ 22 px tall, Sunbelt Part 4 shows the block.
3. **Phone chrome, quiz stages only** (`(max-width: 640px)` and `(max-height: 520px) and (orientation: landscape)`): `body.stage-quiz` / `stage-example` from `stagechange`. Prompt: `.pbody` sr-only except stages 1, 5, 8, 11, 13 until their first `quizopen`; tapping `.ptitle` toggles `#prompt.expanded`, cleared on `quizopen`. Jump bar: 28 px strip (label "Part 2 · 5/14", `#progress`, caret; `::after { inset: -8px 0 }`), portrait `top: calc(var(--prompt-h) + 8px); left: 8px; right: 112px` with `#doorsleft` on the row at `right: 8px; padding: 5px 12px; font-size: 13px`; landscape strip 8,324, 246×28. Tap expands until a tap elsewhere or `stagechange`; timed expand only after stages 3, 6, 9, 11, 13: start on `quizclose`, 3000 ms, next `.jbtn` pulses once. Examples (0, 4, 7, 10, 12) keep the full bar. `#lookbar`: `<button class="lk-menubtn" aria-haspopup aria-expanded>⋯</button>` 44×44 ghost from stage 0, portrait `left: 8px; bottom: 12px`, landscape `top: 8px; right: 8px`, wired to `setOpen`; the menu is the existing list, amber Home first. `#helpbtn` portrait `right: 8px; bottom: 12px`, visible in landscape at the strip's right end. Portrait `#resetview` and `#viewbar` `bottom: 64px`, `#minimap` `bottom: 116px`; landscape `#minimap { top: 60px; right: 8px }`. `body.quiz-open` on phones: strip, `?`, hint, reset chip hidden; `#doorsleft` sr-only.
4. **Reward.** `fireConfetti` origin = solved plate's projected position (fallback: sheet top-centre when the plate is under the sheet); 90 pieces on `pointer: coarse`, 170 otherwise; `confetti.length = 0` on `stagechange`; cannons on stages 3, 6, 9, 11, 13 (finale unchanged).
5. **Prompt copy.** On `stagecomplete`: title `Building ${n} ✓`, no body, all layouts. `nameBuilding` body: "Now the building number — read the leading digits." Coarse-pointer verbs: "Tap".
6. **Landscape column.** `#qopts { display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; gap: 8px }`, `button.opt { min-height: 48px }`, `.num { font-size: 20px }`, `#qmsg` under `#qhead` (`order: -1; min-height: 20px`); `short` under the landscape query; `setPrompt(promptFor(currentStage))` on `resize`.
7. **Hints.** `#draghint` portrait `bottom: 70px; top: auto`, landscape `top: calc(var(--prompt-h) + 8px)`; idle tip once per session (module flag), hidden on `quizopen`; coarse wording "Drag to look round · Pinch to zoom · Tap a glowing ? door to answer".
8. **Touch.** Slop 12 px for touch in the `pointerup` handler; screen-space snap ≤ 28 px to an unsolved plate; halo 1.3× / 1.5× on `(pointer: coarse)`.
9. **Part 5 camera.** Spherical interpolation in `flyTo` when both poses share the target; `updateResetChip` stays hidden while a `SEQ_VIEWS` view is marked.
10. **Desktop:** items 1, 4, 5, 9 only.

Apply every CSS/DOM/JS item to all four apartment files; only the neighbour move and `DECOR_BOXES.push` are Sunbelt-specific.

**Do not:** hide or auto-hide `#bottombar`; put a second Next inside the sheet; go below 44 px on any control or 15/13 px on copy, or shrink the 2×2 grid; peek the part buttons per building; add edge-swipes, long-presses or tap-the-sky reveals; dolly on every tap; animate the prompt's height; tween the view offset back on `quizclose`; put a timer on the prompt body; re-arm the idle tip per stage; keep amber Home on the surface; touch the worked examples' chrome beyond the layout-wide ⋯ button; change `STAGES` or the option logic.

— Rin Castellanos, senior game developer and designer
